About Move ♥ Me

for love, for work, or both.

This experience is for those who want to find love, or a job, or maybe both. Studies probably show that people are willing to move for love, or move for a job. And now more than ever, I’m willing to bet people are getting a bit more desperate, a lot more bold. I believe many young people are willing to move to a new state to find love and start a relationship, or find jobs and start their career. Heck, mid-career job switchers, divorcees, recent widowers, and workaholics finally looking to settle down, are all just as likely to jump state borders.

We love love, we love our careers. But both the job market and the dating scene are bleak at the moment (iykyk)..

This tool was created for the curious love seekers and career builders. The ones who’d actually book a trip for a date, or a job interview, or both.

Are you one of them? Check out this map and take the Move ♥ Me intro quiz to see what places in the world (well, in America) are most likely to move you..
